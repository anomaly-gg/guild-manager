Is there a free trial?

Yes. Sign in with Discord or Google and start a 7-day Premium trial from your account menu. No payment details needed, one trial per account.

Who needs to pay, the leader or every member?

Only the person who leads the team. Premium is tied to your account, and every team you lead gets the Premium features for all of its members.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

Nothing is deleted. Your team drops back to the Free limits: you keep one team, and extra members or timers beyond the Free cap stay in place but can't be added to until you're under the limit again.

How do payments and refunds work?

Checkout is on Gumroad and takes cards and PayPal. After paying you get a license key by email; the app picks it up by itself, or you can paste it in. Monthly plans can be cancelled any time and stop at the end of the paid month. See the refund policy for the details.
